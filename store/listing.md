## Description

45 Days Abs Challenge is a self-guided core training plan.

Open the app and follow the current day. Days 1 to 7 are free. Each of those days lists the exercises, the sets, and a rest time, plus a still illustration of the form. A rest timer counts down between sets. When you finish, mark the day complete. Your streak and a 45-day calendar stay on this device. There is no account to create.

The plan is arranged in four phases:

- Foundation (Days 1-10): form and activation
- Build (Days 11-25): strength and volume
- Intensify (Days 26-40): density and control
- Peak (Days 41-45): the last sessions of the plan

Days 8 to 45, including the later recovery days, unlock with Pro.
