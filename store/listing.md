**Description**
A practical, progressive 45-day core training plan.

The app gives you a clear daily workout that gets more demanding across four phases:
- Foundation (Days 1-10) — Form and Activation
- Build (Days 11-25) — Strength and Volume
- Intensify (Days 26-40) — Density and Control
- Peak (Days 41-45) — Finish Strong

Days 1 to 7 are free. The rest of the plan is available with Pro.
