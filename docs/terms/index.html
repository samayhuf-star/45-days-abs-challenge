The app

45 Days Abs Challenge is a self-guided 45-day core training plan. Days 1 to 7 are free. Later days require a Pro purchase (monthly, yearly, or lifetime).

Subscriptions and payments

Monthly ($2.99/month) and yearly ($19.99/year) plans include a 7-day free trial. Lifetime is $39.99 and has no trial. Prices may be shown in your local currency by the App Store.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings, Apple Account, Subscriptions.

Paid subscriptions are also subject to Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement: Apple EULA.

Restore purchases

Use Restore Purchases in the app to recover a Pro entitlement on a device signed in with the same Apple Account.

Health and safety

This app is for general fitness information only. It does not provide medical advice and does not promise specific results. Consult a professional if you are unsure whether this plan is right for you. Stop if you feel pain.
